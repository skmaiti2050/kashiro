Subject: Changes to the expense policy from 1 April

Dear team,

From 1 April, every claim above Rs 5,000 needs a scanned bill and the approval of your reporting manager. Claims of Rs 1,25,000 or more also go to Meera Krishnan in Finance. The new limits follow the review that Arjun Deshpande and Fatima Sheikh finished last month.

Please read the updated SOPs before you file your next claim. The main points are:

a. Travel within the city is paid at actuals, up to Rs 2,000 a day.
b. Hotel stays are booked through the travel desk, not by you.
(iv) Meals during client visits are covered only with a GST invoice.
1. Claims older than sixty days are not paid.

If you are out of station on the last working day of the month, ask your manager to submit the claim for you. We have also decided to prepone the quarterly review to 18 March, so that the new limits are in place before the financial year ends.

The finance team had had several complaints about late payments. The fact that that happened twice in one quarter is why the approval now goes to two people. What it is is a question of trust, and we want everyone to be paid on time.

Payments are made through UPI or NEFT within seven working days. Your KYC details must match the name on your bank account. If they do not, the payment will be returned and you will have to file the claim again.

For questions, write to finance@example.in or call extension 2204. Please log in in the morning, between 9:30 and 11:00, when the help desk is staffed.
