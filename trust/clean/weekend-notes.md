# Notes from a weekend in Pune

We reached Pune on Friday night and stayed with my cousin-brother in Kothrud. On Saturday morning we walked to the Sunday market, which, oddly, opens on Saturdays too. The vegetables were fresh, the crowd was cheerful and the prices were fair.

My aunt asked me to turn the geyser on on Monday, before the plumber came. I said I would, and then I forgot. What it was was a busy weekend, not a lack of care.

## What we ate

- Misal at a small place near the station, where the owner has run the same kitchen for thirty years.
- Vada pav from a cart outside the college.
- A plate of sabudana khichdi that my aunt made for the fast.

The bill for lunch for four came to ₹860, which felt like a bargain after a month of city prices.
