# Hybrid Work Policy

**Policy owner:** Human Resources. **Approved by:** Mr. Vivek Chandra, Chief People Officer. **Effective from:** 1 November 2025. **Applies to:** all full-time employees in India, except roles listed in Section 4.

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how and when employees may work from home, how we expect teams to organise their time in the office, and what support the company provides for remote work. It replaces the interim work-from-home guidelines issued in March 2022.

Our goal are simple. We want people to do their best work, to collaborate well with their colleagues, and to have a reasonable balance between work and the rest of their lives. We believe a mix of office and remote work, planned by each team, is the best way to achieve this.

## 2. The Basic Arrangement

Employees covered by this policy will work from the office on at least three days each week and may work from home on the remaining days. Each team will agree on two "anchor days" when everyone is expected in the office. Anchor days should be used for meetings, planning, training and work that benefits from being in the same room.

Managers will confirm their team's anchor days with HR by 15 October 2025. Once agreed, anchor days should not change more than once a quarter, so that employees can plan their commute, childcare and other commitments.

The third office day is flexible. Employees can choose it based on their own work and there team's needs, as long as they inform their manager in advance through the attendance system.

## 3. Working from Home

When working from home, employees are expected to:

- Be available during the team's core hours, which are 10:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. unless the team agrees otherwise.
- Respond to messages and calls within a reasonable time, as they would in the office.
- Join video calls with their camera on for team meetings, unless there is a good reason not to.
- Work from a place that is safe, quiet and suitable for confidential conversations.
- Follow the company's information security policy, including the rules on using company laptops and VPN.

Working from home is not a substitute for childcare or leave. If an employee is unwell or has personal commitments that prevent them from working, they should apply for the appropriate leave.

## 4. Roles Not Covered

Some roles need to be performed on on site. These include roles in our warehouses and service centres, front-desk and facilities roles, and certain IT infrastructure roles. Employees in these roles will continue too follow the attendance rules set by their department. HR will share a full list of roles not covered with managers.

## 5. Working from Another City

Employees may occasionally work from another city in India, for example while visiting family, for up to 15 working days in a calendar year. This requires approval from their manager at least one week in advance. During this time, the employee must still be available during core hours and must attend any anchor-day meetings by video.

Working from outside India is not permitted under this policy, because it can create tax, legal and data protection issues for both the employee and the company. Requests for exceptions must be approved by the Chief People Officer and the legal team.

## 6. Equipment and Allowances

The company will provide each eligible employee with a laptop, a headset and, on request, an external monitor for home use. Employees who work from home at least one day a week will receive a monthly internet allowance of ₹1,200, paid with their salary.

Employees may also claim a one-time home office allowance of up to ₹10,000 for a desk or chair, with receipts, within six months of this policy taking effect. Claims should be submitted through the expense portal. Equipment bought with this allowance belongs to the employee.

## 7. Health and Safety

Working from home should be safe and confortable. Employees should set up a workspace with good lighting and a chair that supports their back. HR will share a short guide on setting up a home workspace, and employees can request a video consultation with an ergonomics specialist through the wellness programme.

If an employee is injured while working from home, they should report it to their manager and HR within 48 hours, in the same way as an injury in the office.

## 8. Performance and Fairness

Performance will be judged on results, not on where the work is done. Managers must make sure that employees who work from home more often are not disadvantaged in assignments, feedback or promotions. HR will review promotion and rating data each year to check for any bias against employees who use the hybrid arrangement more.

Managers who have concerns about an employee's performance or availability while working from home should discuss them directly with the employee first. If the concerns continue, the manager may with HR's agreement, ask the employee to work from the office more often for a defined period.

## 9. Special Circumstances

We recognise that some employees may need more flexibility for a period of time, for example because of health conditions, pregnancy, disability or caring responsibilities. These employees may request a different arrangement, including working from home on more days. Requests should be made to HR, who will treat them confidentially and respond within 10 working days.

Employees returning from maternity leave may work from home for up to four days a week for the first six months after their return, if their role allows it. This is in addition to any benefits under the Maternity Benefit Act.

## 10. Office Etiquette on Anchor Days

Because anchor days will be busy busy, we ask everyone to:

- Book a desk or meeting room through the workplace app before arriving.
- Keep personal calls short and take them in the phone booths.
- Clear their desk at teh end of the day so that others can use it.

The cafeteria will offer subsidised lunch on anchor days, and the transport desk will run additional shuttle buses from the main metro stations.

## 11. Review

HR will review this policy after six six months, using feedback from an employee survey and data on attendance, engagement and attrition. Any changes will be shared with all employees at least one month before they take effect.

## 12. Questions

Questions about this policy should be sent to the HR helpdesk or to your HR business partner. For questions about information security while working remotely, contact the IT service desk.

*This policy does not form part of any employee's contract of employment, and the company may change it in line with business needs and applicable law.*
