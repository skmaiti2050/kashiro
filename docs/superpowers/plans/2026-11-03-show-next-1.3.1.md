# Show Next and Short "Foreign" Passages (v1.3.1) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** "Show next" outlines the passage it moves to and names it in the panel. One- and two-word headings or names in an English document are no longer reported as "not English".

**Architecture:**
- **Task 1 (engine).** `uncheckedPassages` stops reporting foreign *sentences* shorter than 3 words. `findForeignSentences` is unchanged, so the words in those short sentences keep their protection from zero-click fixes (`src/engine/tiers.ts:206`).
- **Task 2 (UI).**
  - `UncheckedPlace` gains the passage's end.
  - `ParagraphRequest` gains a length, so the editor draws the same `cm-focus` ring a card gets.
  - The review panel shows "n of N" and the passage's text.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, React, CodeMirror 6, Vitest 4 (unit + DOM).

**Spec:** The owner's report of 2026-11-03.
- "I clicked show next, I cannot see it anywhere": Show next only scrolls, and the dotted underline is nearly invisible on the dark theme.
- "It is actually all English": in `OneView BRD.docx` the 6 reported passages were "Priyanshu Dubey" (x2), "Amit, Aniruddha", "Reportees" (x2) and "Gamification". Each is a whole one- or two-word paragraph, which `findForeignSentences` judged foreign (more than half its words flagged as spelling).
- Owner decision: ship both fixes as v1.3.1, a patch.

## Review Focus

1. **A short foreign sentence's words must still never be zero-click fixed.** Only the report changes. Pinned in Task 1 with a session test.
2. **A real foreign passage still counts:** a 3+ word romanised sentence ("ami kal te jabo.") and any other-script run, even a single Devanagari word. Pinned in Task 1.
3. **The ring lands on the right text after the document was edited above the passage** (offsets shift). Pinned in Task 2.
4. **A long passage** is shortened in the panel line, not dumped whole. Pinned in Task 2.
5. **Wrap-around:** after the last place, the next one is "1 of N" again, and its ring moves there. Pinned in Task 2.

### Task 2: "Show next" outlines the passage and names it

**Files:**
- Modify: `src/ui/state/unchecked.ts` (`UncheckedPlace`)
- Modify: `src/ui/DocumentView.tsx` (`ParagraphRequest` and its effect)
- Modify: `src/ui/App.tsx`, wherever it builds a `ParagraphRequest` from `onShowParagraph`
- Modify: `src/ui/ReviewPanel.tsx` (`showNextUnchecked` and the line under it)
- Modify: `src/ui/i18n/en.json`, `hi.json`, `bn.json`
- Test: `tests/dom/notChecked.test.tsx`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `UncheckedPassage.span` and `session.otherScriptParagraphs()`, unchanged.
- Produces:
  - `UncheckedPlace` = `{ paraId: number; offset: number; length: number }`. For a set-aside paragraph, `length` is the paragraph's text length; for a passage, it is `span.end - span.start`.
  - `ParagraphRequest` gains `length: number`.
  - `onShowParagraph(paraId, offset, length)`.
  - The i18n key `panel.shownOther`, with these exact strings:
    - en: `"{at} of {total}: “{text}”"`
    - hi: `"{total} में से {at}: “{text}”"`
    - bn: `"{total}টির মধ্যে {at}: “{text}”"`

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests.** Extend `tests/dom/notChecked.test.tsx`, reusing its `MIXED_DOCUMENT` and helpers:
  - `'Show next outlines the place it moves to, like a card'`. After a click, the editor's `.cm-focus` element's text equals the first place's text. After a second click, it equals the second place's text.
  - `'Show next names the place: "1 of 2" and its text, and wraps to 1 after the last'`.
  - `'a long passage is shortened in the panel line'`. A Hindi paragraph longer than 40 characters makes the line show its first 40 characters followed by `…`.
  - `'after an edit above it, the outline still covers the passage'`:
    - type a word into the first English paragraph;
    - wait for the re-check;
    - click Show next;
    - `.cm-focus` text equals the other-script paragraph's text.

- [ ] **Step 2: Run them; they fail.** `npx vitest run tests/dom/notChecked.test.tsx`

- [ ] **Step 3: Implement.**
  - `uncheckedPlaces` fills in `length`.
  - The `paragraphRequest` effect in `DocumentView` dispatches `setFocusMark` with `FOCUS_MARK.range(from, to)`, plus the existing `scrollIntoView`. That is the same ring a card gets. Focus stays on the button.
  - `ReviewPanel` remembers the last place shown, as an index. Under the count line, it renders a `<p className="hint" aria-live="polite">` with `panel.shownOther`, where:
    - `at` is the 1-based index;
    - `total` is `places.length`;
    - `text` is the place's text, cut to 40 characters plus `…` when longer.
  - The line disappears when `places` changes length (a new check).

- [ ] **Step 4: Run the tests; they pass.** Same command.

- [ ] **Step 5: Gates, one at a time:** `npm run test:changed`, then `npm run test:dom`.

- [ ] **Step 6: Commit** as `fix(ui): Show next outlines the passage it moves to and names it`.
